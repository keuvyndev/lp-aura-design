# Project Brief: Studio Aura Landing Page

## 1. Project Overview

**Client:** Marina Vasconcelos, Studio Aura
**Industry:** High-end Interior Design & Architecture
**Objective:** Create an immersive, sophisticated landing page that communicates "discreet luxury" and converts high-net-worth leads.

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy

- **Concept:** "The Curated Void" – Balancing material richness with spatial breath.
- **Visual Direction:** Boutique hotel meets art gallery. Minimalist, high-contrast, and focused on texture and light.
- **Color Palette (Studio Aura Design System):**
  - Primary: Deep Onyx (#0f0f0f) / Charcoal (#121414)
  - Accent: Champagne Gold (used for highlights and call-to-actions)
  - Neutral: Off-white/Silver for high-readability typography.
- **Typography:** Bodoni Moda (Serif) for headings to evoke timeless elegance; Sans-serif for clean, functional body copy.

## 3. Experience & Sitemap

The page is designed as a single-page immersive journey:

1.  **Hero (Opening):** "A Essência do Luxo Discreto." Features a background video (`interior-dark-video.mp4`) to set an immediate atmospheric tone.
2.  **Our Process:** Three-step narrative (Consultoria, Conceito, Execução) paired with technical video context (`video-camadas.mp4`).
3.  **Selected Projects (Portfolio):** A refined, asymmetrical gallery showcasing 7 high-end residential projects with varied aspect ratios.
4.  **The Founder:** "Marina Vasconcelos" – Personal storytelling section to build trust and authority, featuring professional portraiture.
5.  **Testimonials:** Social proof from high-profile clients in São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro.
6.  **Contact/Lead Gen:** Dual-entry form for project inquiries and a direct WhatsApp integration for immediate consultation.

## 4. Technical Requirements

- **Platform:** AI-native high-fidelity HTML/CSS.
- **Responsiveness:** Desktop-first with fluid transitions; mobile-optimized for luxury on-the-go viewing.
- **Performance:** Optimized video assets for fast loading without quality loss.
- **Interactions:** Subtle fade-ins, parallax effects on imagery, and smooth scroll transitions to enhance the "luxury experience" feel.

## 5. Assets Inventory

- `interior-dark-video.mp4` (Hero background)
- `video-camadas.mp4` (Process section)
- Portfolio: 7 curated interior images.
- Founder Portrait: Professional studio shot of Marina Vasconcelos.
